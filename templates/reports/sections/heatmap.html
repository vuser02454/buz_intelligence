How to interpret this – and its limits

  • Levels are thresholds on the density of mapped OpenStreetMap features in a sector (High ≥ {{ thresholds.high }}/km², Medium {{ thresholds.medium }}–{{ thresholds.high_minus_one }}/km², Low below {{ thresholds.medium }}/km²; outer sectors are larger, so density is used rather than raw counts), checked against the code in business_intelligence/analysis.py.
  • Features are shops, food, services, offices, leisure and transit stops (street furniture such as benches is excluded).
  • The heatmap does not count people. Well-mapped areas can look busier than they are, and poorly mapped areas quieter.
  • The website footer's "Crowd Score" ({{ s.heatmap.website_crowd_score }}) is the number of features returned, capped at 100. It is not a measure of people.
