Method. Each nearby place counts fully within 300 m and linearly less up to 1.5 km (weight 0.2 at the edge). Complementary businesses and demand generators each have a relevance to {{ y.business }}; a relationship's strength is its distance-weighted count divided by a saturation level, capped at 1. The competition factor falls as distance-weighted similar places within 1 km ({{ y.competition.pressure }}) approach the capacity the local demand supports ({{ y.competition.capacity }}), with a bonus for destination clusters and a penalty for saturation within 400 m. Accessibility is the revenue engine's own accessibility score. Score = Σ weight × factor.
